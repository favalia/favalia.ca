Inki Run

Draw lines. Save the runner.

Your runner never jumps. You draw the way: bridges over holes, ramps over rocks and trees, roofs against falling stones and walls that stop birds and cars.

How it works

Draw it.
He runs on it.

Lines only carry your runner from above. From the side he passes right through them, so a wall stops a car but never stops him.

Holes

Draw a bridge from edge to edge.

Rocks & trees

Start on the ground and draw a ramp up and over.

Spikes & cacti

Go over the top. If your line clears it, you're safe.

Falling stones

Draw a roof above your runner's head.

Birds & cars

Draw a wall. They bounce off; your runner passes right through.

Time slows down while your finger is on the screen. Every line costs ink, so grab stars to earn more.

Questions

Good to know

My line was right there, but I still crashed
  • Start ramps on the ground before the obstacle: your runner can't jump up onto a line.
  • Lines only carry your runner from above. From the side, he passes through them.
  • Keep ramps gentle when riding: bikes and segways can't climb steep lines.
  • Time slows down while your finger is on the screen, so take a moment.
I ran out of ink

Every line costs ✎1. Keep running, grab stars (★) and block stones, birds and cars with your lines to earn more.

What does a ride do?

A ride takes one hit for you: it smashes, your runner flips over the obstacle and keeps running without losing a heart. Falling into a hole still costs a heart.

When do ads appear?

An ad is shown after every 5 finished stages, won or lost. In Endless mode, after a crash you can choose to watch an ad to continue your run, up to twice per run. Ads never interrupt a run.

Where is my progress saved?

Stages, stars, your best Endless distance and your outfits are saved on your device. They come back with an iCloud or computer backup of it, but deleting the app deletes them. There is no account.
