PLAYING

How do I play?

Your runner runs by itself and never jumps. Drag your finger to draw lines: a bridge over a hole, a ramp over a rock or tree, a roof against falling stones, a wall against birds and cars. Tap ? on the menu for the How to Play with live demos.

My line was right there, but I still crashed

  • Start ramps on the ground before the obstacle: your runner can't jump up onto a line.
  • Lines only carry your runner from above. From the side, your runner passes through them.
  • Keep ramps gentle when riding: bikes and segways can't climb steep lines.
  • Time slows down while your finger is on the screen, so take a moment.

I ran out of ink

Every line costs ✎1. Keep running, grab stars (★) and block stones, birds and cars with your lines to earn more.

What does a ride do?

A ride takes one hit for you: it smashes, your runner flips over the obstacle and keeps running without losing a heart. Falling into a hole still costs a heart.

ADS

When do ads appear?

An ad is shown after every 5 finished stages, won or lost. In Endless mode, after a crash you can choose to watch an ad to continue your run, up to twice per run. Ads never interrupt a run; the Endless one only plays if you choose it.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn Inki Run off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can also change your choices from AD CHOICES in the game's Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

PROGRESS & SETTINGS

Where is my progress saved?

Stages, stars, your best Endless distance and your outfits are saved on your device. They come back with an iCloud or computer backup of it, but deleting the app deletes them. There is no account.

No sound?

Check that sound is ON in the game's Settings (the gear on the menu). On iPhone, the game follows the silent switch.

Notifications

With notifications on, you get a few reminders after you stop playing (a day, 3 days, a week and two weeks later, never at night, then they stop) and the occasional game news, such as new stages. Turn them on or off with NOTIFICATIONS in the game's Settings. If they won't turn on, allow notifications for Inki Run in your device's Settings › Notifications.

Light or dark?

In the game's Settings, THEME can be DAY, NIGHT or AUTO (follows your device's appearance).
