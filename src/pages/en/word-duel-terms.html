Word Duel is a word game, free to play. Play fairly: no automated play, no interfering with other players' duels, and no trying to get at other people's accounts. Accounts that do are closed.

You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself. Pick a player name others can live with: names that impersonate someone, or that are there to abuse, will be changed or removed. You can delete your player at any time from the app, and that removes everything with it.

  • Coins are part of the game, not money. They buy extra hearts. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, cannot be transferred between players, and are not refundable by us.

The monthly plan renews itself until you cancel it in your Apple Account or Google Play subscriptions, and cancelling stops the next payment rather than refunding the current one. The one-off unlock never renews. Both belong to the account that bought them and can be restored on your other devices with the same store, and on iPhone and iPad the one-off unlock is shared with your Family Sharing group.

  • Coins bought in the app stay with the account they were bought on; deleting the account ends them.
