Questions

Good to know

The game slowed down and paused

That happens when you lift your finger: time slows down, then the game pauses. Put your finger back on the screen to fly on. Leaving the app also pauses the game.

How do fuel and lives work?
  • Fuel drains while you fly. Catch parachute fuel cans and drums, and fly under the Sky Pump balloon to refill. An empty tank ends the stage.
  • Lives are the small planes at the top left. Every hit costs one; the last one means you're shot down. An armor plate takes a hit for you.
What do the weapon letters mean?

M Twin Nib, S Splatter, H Scribble, C Ink Cannon, L Quill Beam, Z Zigzag Bolt. Catching the same letter again powers it up.

How do I open the next world?

Beat the world's boss (stage 10) and collect enough stars: 20 stars for every world you've finished. Replay stages to earn all three stars.

When do ads appear?

Ads only come between stages, never during a flight, and not in your first few stages. Every 7 minutes the game offers a deal: watch one short ad and you won't see any other ads for the next 7 minutes.
