Privacy policy

Website privacy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The short version

  • Analytics (Google Analytics) and marketing tools are off until you agree in the cookie banner.
  • Without your consent, we don't collect anything that identifies you.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • Change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

1. Who we are

Favalia is an independent game studio in Montréal, Québec, Canada. This policy covers the website favalia.ca. Our games have their own policies: Word Duel, Inki Run and Inki Ace.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the founder of Favalia. Contact: supportwordduel@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Without your consent

Nothing that identifies you. The site remembers a few things in your own browser (local storage): your cookie choice, the language and day/night theme you picked and your best scores in the game demos. They never leave your device and you can clear them in your browser settings.

Like any website, some services see your IP address when your browser loads the page: our host GitHub Pages (which keeps server logs for security), Google Fonts (the typefaces) and Apple (the “Download on the App Store” badge). See the GitHub, Google and Apple privacy policies.

With your consent: analytics

If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, time spent, clicks on links (for example to the App Store), the site you came from, device and browser type, screen size, language and approximate location (city and country, worked out from your IP address; Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses). We use it only to improve the site and to see which games interest people.

With your consent: marketing

If you accept marketing and we run ad campaigns, Google's advertising tags (through Google Tag Manager) may record whether an ad led to your visit. This is off unless you turn it on.

4. Where your data goes and how long we keep it

Google and GitHub process data on servers outside Québec and Canada, mainly in the United States, under their own privacy and data-protection commitments. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Consent: accept, refuse or change your choice at any time with Cookie settings. Refusing never blocks any part of the site.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent. Because we don't keep personal information about website visitors ourselves, analytics data is best handled through Google, but write to us and we'll help.
  • Other tools: you can also block cookies in your browser or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Complaints: you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can contact your local data-protection authority.

6. Children

This website isn't directed at children under 14, and analytics only runs if someone accepts it in the cookie banner.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the change affects what you agreed to, we'll ask for your consent again.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: supportwordduel@gmail.com
