Draw lines. Reach your puppy.

Your runner never jumps, and only wants one thing: to get home to their puppy. You hold the pencil. Draw bridges over holes, ramps over rocks, shields against falling stones and walls that stop cars.

Borij means “run!” in Mazandarani.

How it works

Draw it.
They run on it.

Lines only carry your runner from above. From the side, your runner passes right through them, so a wall stops a taxi but never stops your runner.

Holes & ponds

Draw a bridge from edge to edge.

Rocks & trees

Start on the ground and draw a ramp up and over.

Spikes & cacti

Go over the top. If your line clears it, you're safe.

Stones & snowballs

Draw a shield above your runner's head.

Birds & taxis

Draw a wall. They bounce off; your runner passes right through.

Time slows down while your finger is on the screen. Every line costs ink: grab the pens on the way, and earn more the further you run.

The goal

Run home
to your puppy.

At the end of every mission, your puppy is waiting. The reunion is the best part: a run, a leap, a big hug.

  • 🐾

    Name it. Biscuit is only the start.

  • 🎨

    Pick its coat. Eight of them, from golden to dalmatian and midnight.

  • 🎀

    Dress it up. Collars, bandanas, a bow tie, a bell, and hats from a bow to a crown.

  • 💨

    Chase it. In Marathon your puppy runs ahead every 300 m, leaping everything in its way.

Questions

Good to know

My line was right there, but I still crashed
  • Start ramps on the ground before the obstacle: your runner can't jump up onto a line.
  • Lines only carry your runner from above. From the side, your runner passes through them.
  • Keep ramps gentle when riding: bikes and segways can't climb steep lines.
  • Still stuck? SHOW ME THE WAY shows every line the mission needs.
I ran out of ink

Every line costs ✎1. Grab the pens on the way (a rare ×3 pen is worth three), and the further you run, the more ink you earn: at 50 m, 100 m, 200 m and beyond.

When do ads appear?

Never during a run, and not at all in your first 8 missions. After that, a full-screen ad plays after every 3 finished missions and every 2 Marathon runs. Other ads only play if you choose them: to keep running after your last life, to see the way, to double your treats or to try something on for one run.

What is BORIJ PLUS?

An optional subscription, $19.99 a year or $3.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country): no ads at all, double treats, and 40% of the wardrobe unlocked. If you only want the full-screen ads gone, Remove Ads is a single $4.99 purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Missions, stars, treats, your wardrobe and your puppy are saved on your device. They come back with an iCloud or computer backup of it, but deleting the app deletes them. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases. There is no account.
